하나마이크론은 반도체 후공정 외주(OSAT, Outsourced Semiconductor Assembly and Test) 전문기업으로, 메모리 칩의 패키징(조립)·테스트를 삼성전자·SK하이닉스로부터 위탁받아 수행함.

자회사 하나머티리얼즈(반도체 전공정용 Si·SiC 부품)를 연결로 보유해 소재~후공정 수직 계열 구도를 갖추고 있으며, 브라질·베트남에 해외 생산법인을 두고 현지 공급과 증설을 병행하는 글로벌 생산 체제를 운영함.

FY25 연결 매출 1조5,344억원·영업이익 1,277억원을 기록했으며, Q1 2026에 매출 5,076억원(+64% YoY)·영업이익 720억원(+514% YoY)으로 OSAT 업황 턴어라운드가 실적으로 확인됨.

3.1.OSAT — 반도체 후공정 외주의 구조

반도체는 전공정(웨이퍼 위에 회로를 만드는 공정)과 후공정(완성된 웨이퍼를 칩 단위로 자르고 포장·검사하는 공정)으로 나뉨. 후공정은 패키징(칩 보호·외부 연결 조립)과 테스트(불량 선별)로 구성되며, OSAT은 이를 외주받아 수행하는 사업임.

전공정이 '웨이퍼를 빚는 단계'라면 후공정은 '완성된 칩을 포장하고 검수하는 단계'임. 메모리사(삼성·SK하이닉스)가 전공정(웨이퍼)에 집중하고 후공정 물량을 외주로 돌릴수록 OSAT 매출이 늘어남.

패키징은 와이어본딩 같은 전통 방식부터 플립칩·범프·SiP(시스템인패키지)·2.5D/3D 적층 등 첨단 방식까지 난이도가 다양함. 첨단 패키징일수록 물량당 매출(P)이 높아져 OSAT 수익성에 유리하나, 가장 고난도인 HBM 적층은 메모리사가 직접 내재화하는 비중이 높음.

매출이 메모리사의 후공정 외주 물량에 연동됨. 하나마이크론은 메모리사와 외주 OSAT 사이의 관문에 위치해 외주화 비율이 높아질수록 직접 수혜를 봄. 다만 HBM 같은 고난도 후공정은 IDM이 직접 수행하는 비중이 높아, 하나의 직접 수혜는 주로 범용 D램·낸드 외주 확대에서 나옴.

Q1 2026 실적이 이 수혜 경로를 실증함. 반도체 제조(OSAT) 부문이 전년 동기 대비 +64% 성장하며 영업이익이 1,578% 급증했고, OPM이 역사적 6~8%대에서 12.2%로 도약했음. HBM 집중에 따른 범용 메모리 외주 확대와 베트남 신규 capa 가동률 정상화가 동시에 작용한 결과임.

3.4.전방 수요 동인

OSAT 매출의 1차 동인은 고객(삼성·SK하이닉스)의 메모리 생산량과 후공정 외주 비율임. AI 수요로 메모리사가 HBM·고부가 제품에 전공정 capa를 집중하면, 상대적으로 범용 D램·낸드의 후공정이 OSAT로 외주화되어 하나마이크론 물량이 늘어나는 구조임.

2차 동인은 하나마이크론 자체 증설(베트남 라인 가동)임. 신규 capa가 가동률을 끌어올려야 외주 물량 증가가 매출로 실현됨. 증설은 차입을 동반해 가동 초기에는 감가상각·금융비용이 이익률을 눌렀으며, Q1 2026에서 가동률 정상화 레버리지가 본격 발현 중임.

3Industry Analysis

3.1 OSAT structure · 3.2 Value chain · 3.3 HBM/AI pathways · 3.4 End demand

3.1.OSAT — the structure of semiconductor back-end outsourcing

Semiconductor manufacturing splits into front-end (building circuits on the wafer) and back-end (dicing the finished wafer into chips, then packaging and testing them). The back-end consists of packaging (protecting the chip and assembling external connections) and test (screening defects); OSAT is the business of performing this work on an outsourced basis.

If the front-end is "shaping the wafer," the back-end is "packaging and inspecting the finished chip." The more memory makers (Samsung, SK hynix) concentrate on the front-end (wafers) and push back-end volume out to subcontractors, the more OSAT revenue grows.

Packaging spans a wide range of difficulty, from traditional methods such as wire bonding to advanced approaches including flip-chip, bumping, SiP (system-in-package), and 2.5D/3D stacking. The more advanced the packaging, the higher the revenue per unit of volume (P), which helps OSAT profitability — but HBM stacking, the most demanding of all, is largely internalized by the memory makers themselves.

The semiconductor manufacturing (OSAT) division grew +64% YoY with operating profit surging 1,578%, and OPM jumped from a historical 6–8% range to 12.2%. This was the combined result of expanded commodity memory outsourcing driven by HBM concentration and the normalization of utilization at new Vietnam capacity.

3.4.End-demand drivers

The primary driver of OSAT revenue is the memory output of customers (Samsung, SK hynix) and their back-end outsourcing ratio. As AI demand pushes memory makers to concentrate front-end capacity on HBM and high-value products, the back-end of commodity DRAM and NAND is relatively more outsourced to OSATs, increasing Hana Micron's volume.

The secondary driver is Hana Micron's own capacity expansion (bringing Vietnam lines online). New capacity must lift utilization for higher outsourced volume to translate into revenue. Expansion has been debt-funded, so depreciation and financing costs weighed on margins in the early ramp; in Q1 2026 the leverage from utilization normalization is now coming through in earnest.

7.3.Scenario narratives

Base — utilization normalization, consensus commodity outsourcing expansion

OSAT processed volume of 1,500kwpm (+43% vs the FY25 estimate) × ASP 1.3bn KRW → revenue of 1.95tn KRW, and at 10% OPM, operating profit of 195.0bn KRW. The materials division adds 72.0bn KRW on 400bn KRW of revenue at 18% OPM, helped by the new SiC business. Total operating profit of 267.0bn KRW, controlling net profit of 93.5bn KRW, EPS of 1,408 KRW, and a TP of 28,160 KRW.

This is the scenario in which the new Vietnam lines run normally and the expansion of commodity memory outsourcing driven by IDMs' HBM concentration is absorbed at the level of broker consensus (FY26 operating profit of ~217.3bn KRW). At 33,200 KRW the current price is 18% above the Base TP, so downside risk exists if only the Base case materializes. This is the conservative outcome if OPM improves only modestly, from 8.3% in FY25 to 10%.

Bull — Q1 2026 OPM trend sustained, SiC contribution from materials

OSAT processed volume of 1,800kwpm (+71%) × ASP 1.4bn KRW → revenue of 2.52tn KRW at 13% OPM (a further improvement from the 12.2% OSAT OPM in Q1 2026). The materials division contributes 104.0bn KRW on 520bn KRW at 20% OPM. Total operating profit of 431.6bn KRW, controlling net profit of 151.1bn KRW, EPS of 2,275 KRW, and a TP of 45,500 KRW.

This is the scenario in which the 12.2% OSAT OPM of Q1 2026 rises modestly further to 13% by FY28E. Sustaining fixed-cost leverage requires outsourced volume to keep growing, and this assumption breaks if the memory cycle slows or IDMs expand in-house capacity. At 33,200 KRW the current price is 27% below the Bull TP of 45,500 KRW, meaning the market has not yet priced in the Bull scenario.

Best — SA computing demand surge, outsourcing volume spike

This is the scenario in which surging SA (Sovereign AI) computing demand accelerates Samsung's and SK's HBM concentration, pushing the outsourcing ratio for commodity DRAM and NAND back-end work far above current levels. OSAT processed volume of 2,500kwpm (+138%) × ASP 1.5bn KRW → revenue of 3.75tn KRW at 16% OPM for operating profit of 600.0bn KRW. Hana Materials expands its global SiC share, contributing 143.0bn KRW on 650bn KRW at 22% OPM. Total operating profit of 743.0bn KRW and a TP of 78,360 KRW (+136%).

The premise of the Best scenario is that the OSAT outsourcing ratio rises from roughly 25% of IDMs' total back-end volume to over 40%, and that Hana Micron establishes itself as a principal consignee for Korean volume. Since Hana Micron is a challenger with single-digit global share, one must account for the fact that a spike in outsourced volume would be shared with top-tier players such as ASE and Amkor.

7.4.Risk factors

  • One-off nature of other non-operating income: If the 37.1bn KRW of other non-operating income in Q1 2026 consists of FX-related and similar one-off items, controlling net profit would fall sharply once it normalizes. A conservative stance is warranted until the sustainability of the 1,019 KRW quarterly EPS is confirmed.
  • Financing-cost burden: FY25 financial costs of 88.0bn KRW (including 65.3bn KRW of interest expense) amount to about 69% of operating profit. If rates rise or borrowings increase, the controlling net-profit conversion rate could fall back below the FY25 level of 29.8%.
  • Memory cycle: As in FY23 — 6.0% OPM and −13.5bn KRW controlling net profit — a slowdown hits OSAT utilization directly, and the business structure can swing to a loss.
  • Customer concentration and in-house risk: If Samsung and SK internalize back-end work or reduce outsourcing, volume takes a direct hit. Share would also weaken if top-tier OSATs absorb Korean volume in advanced packaging (HBM stacking and the like).

8Conclusion

Turnaround confirmed · challenger structure · structural net-profit dilution

Q1 2026 OSAT operating profit of 50.7bn KRW (12.2% OPM) confirms the turnaround in reported earnings. The share price has since corrected from its May high of 52,900 KRW to 33,200 KRW, leaving it 27% below the FY28E Bull scenario (TP 45,500 KRW, premised on 13% OPM and +71% processed volume).

Viewed within the value chain, Hana Micron has "a challenger structure in which Q can grow but P gains are limited." Weak negotiating power on outsourcing rates means much of the volume growth disperses into revenue expansion rather than margin improvement. Moreover, the structure in which interest expense (65.3bn KRW in FY25) and non-controlling interests structurally dilute the controlling shareholders' share of net profit remains intact, so the margin of safety depends on whether the Bull scenario or better is realized.

Upside is +37% if the Bull scenario (TP 45,500 KRW) materializes and +136% under the Best scenario (an SA computing demand surge driving an outsourcing spike, TP 78,360 KRW). Normalization of other non-operating income, the pace of decline in financing costs, and Hana Materials' expansion of SiC share are the key monitoring indicators.

## 1. Company Overview

_Founding & business overview · 2026 structural reshaping_

Dongjin Semichem, founded in 1973, is Korea's only manufacturer of photoresist (light-sensitive coating) for semiconductors and displays. In 1989 it became the first in Korea to develop photoresist in-house, leading the localization of an import-dependent material, and it listed on KOSDAQ in 1999.

Its products center on exposure-process photoresist and span chemical materials across semiconductor and display processes — anti-reflective coating (BARC), SOC, CMP slurry, Wet Chemical, and color resist. Headquartered in Incheon, it operates production facilities in Hwaseong, Siheung, and Eumseong.

FY25 continuing-operations revenue was 1,194.1bn KRW with operating profit of 172.4bn KRW (OPM 14.4%). 1Q26 revenue was 328.1bn KRW with operating profit of 66.6bn KRW and OPM 20.3%, a phase in which the semiconductor-materials cycle recovery is being reflected in earnings.

The 2026 structure changes significantly. The blowing-agent business was spun off (Dongjin Innochem) and ten Chinese display subsidiaries were classified as held for sale. The two moves reshape the company into a pure-play domestic semiconductor/display materials company.

## 3. Industry Analysis

_3.1 How photoresist works · 3.2 Japan-dominated market · 3.3 EUV / HBM CMP options_

We examine, in order: how photoresist works (basics) → the Japan-dominated market structure (market) → the company's external options of EUV localization and HBM CMP (options).

### 3.1. How Photoresist Works

Photoresist is a light-sensitive chemical that reacts to light, the core material of the exposure process that etches fine circuit patterns onto semiconductor and display surfaces. This photosensitive film is applied to a wafer and exposed to circuit-shaped light; the chemical properties of the light-struck and unstruck areas differ, printing the circuit.

The finer the circuit line width, the shorter the wavelength of light required, and each wavelength needs a dedicated photoresist. In order of shortening wavelength: g-line/i-line (display/legacy) → KrF → ArF (fine semiconductors) → EUV (7nm and below, cutting-edge); the shorter the wavelength, the more sharply material difficulty and value-add rise.

EUV (extreme ultraviolet) photoresist is a cutting-edge material reacting to a 13.5nm wavelength; with high light energy and low exposure dose, molecular-level uniformity and sensitivity control are extremely demanding. This difficulty is itself the entry barrier, and only a handful of global companies hold the mass-production technology.

**Key:** Dongjin Semichem is Korea's only volume manufacturer of semiconductor/display photoresist. In areas with low material-localization rates, the scarcity of a Korean company making it directly is the basis of the company's premium, but the highest-value-add EUV photoresist is still an area Japan holds 95%+ of, so the company's share is at an early stage.

### 3.2. Japan-Dominated Market Structure

Photoresist is a symbolic bottleneck of material localization. Japan's JSR, Tokyo Ohka Kogyo (TOK), and Shin-Etsu Chemical hold about 70% of the total market and 95% of the high-purity segment, and it was one of the top three items first cited during Japan's 2019 export controls on Korea.

For EUV photoresist, on a 2023 shipment basis, four Japanese companies — TOK 31.6%, Shin-Etsu 30.2%, and others — hold over 95%. The company has entered this area as Korea's sole player, but its absolute share is still a negligible early stage.

In display photoresist and color resist, and in non-EUV areas such as ArF/KrF, the company has already secured localization competitiveness and supplies domestic customers including Samsung, SK, and LG. That is, the company's core business is already-validated non-EUV/display materials, and EUV is a growth option layered on top.

### 3.3. EUV / HBM CMP Options

The substance of the company's share-price premium is three options. All point in a favorable direction, but their revenue contribution is still early, and this report reflects them only conservatively in the §6 valuation.

First, EUV photoresist localization. Prompted by Japan's 2019 export controls, it began development and mass-produced an EUV negative photoresist, with a history of partial deployment in Samsung Electronics' DRAM process. It is also pursuing supply to Samsung foundry EUV lines and is cooperating with SK Hynix on EUV PR localization, attempting to move beyond "Samsung concentration." The direction breaks Japan's monopoly, but expanding mass-production share is a work in progress.

Second, HBM CMP slurry. CMP slurry is an abrasive that planarizes the wafer surface; the company has newly entered supply for SK Hynix HBM, partly encroaching on the existing monopoly structure. HBM 16–24-layer stacking and AI-server demand pull up slurry volume. The CMP slurry market is estimated at ~$2.5–3.0bn in 2025.

Third, a U.S. production base. Through a Texas local subsidiary it is pursuing in-U.S. material production, with customer diversification and possible local subsidies cited as long-term options. However, the timing and scale of monetization are undetermined.

### 4.3. Pricing Power

The company's pricing power is mid-level. The strength of localization scarcity and the weakness of dependence on a few large customers coexist.

The strength is localization scarcity. As the only domestic volume producer, customers need the company for supply stability and supply-chain dual-sourcing.

The weakness is EUV dependence. In cutting-edge EUV, four Japanese companies hold 95%, so customers can procure Japanese product even without the company.

OPM tells the position. The company's OPM of 11–15% is lower than a monopoly-material maker's (40%+) and higher than a general chemical-material maker's — an upper-mid margin. If EUV share rises, upside opens, but until then bargaining power swings with the cycle.

### 6.3. Scenario Narratives

#### Base — cycle recovery, EUV/HBM early

The semiconductor core business grows on the materials cycle from 784.6bn KRW in FY25 to 980.0bn KRW in FY28, cumulative +25% (+8%/yr) over three years. Adding only new EUV photoresist 20.0bn and HBM CMP 30.0bn gives semiconductors 1,030.0bn. With display 320.0bn and other 105.0bn, total revenue is 1,455.0bn, total operating profit 219.4bn, net profit 171.1bn, and EPS 3,328 KRW. Applying an 18x P/E gives a target price of 59,900 KRW (+39.1%), above the current price.

#### Bull — EUV line adoption + HBM CMP ramp

The core business grows +9%/yr to 1,020.0bn, and with Samsung/SK EUV-line adoption progressing, new EUV 100.0bn and HBM CMP slurry 80.0bn are added for semiconductors of 1,200.0bn. With display 350.0bn and other 110.0bn, total revenue is 1,660.0bn, total operating profit 252.8bn, net profit 197.2bn, EPS 3,835 KRW. Applying an 18x P/E gives a target price of 69,000 KRW (+60.3%).

#### Best — full-scale EUV penetration, eroding Japan's share

On a core business of 1,060.0bn, with EUV photoresist penetrating mass-production lines in earnest, new EUV 250.0bn and HBM CMP 150.0bn are added, expanding semiconductors to 1,460.0bn. With display 380.0bn and other 120.0bn, total revenue is 1,960.0bn, total operating profit 302.8bn, net profit 236.2bn, EPS 4,594 KRW. Applying an 18x P/E gives a target price of 82,700 KRW (+92.1%).

Fixing OPM at 18% is the key conservative assumption. New EUV revenue has a favorable mix and could actually lift OPM, in which case Best would be higher. Conversely, if EUV/HBM adoption is delayed and the semiconductor cycle slows, even Base would be revised down.

The condition that breaks the verdict is clear. If the adoption share of the company's photoresist on Samsung/SK EUV lines officially expands, or if HBM CMP slurry share rises into double digits, the conservative assumptions must be raised. Until then, the current multiple is viewed as a zone that has pre-reflected option value.

### 6.4. Risk Factors

- **EUV-share uncertainty** — cutting-edge EUV photoresist is held 95% by four Japanese companies. If the company's mass-production share expansion is delayed, the EUV increment and the premium rationale weaken.
- **Valuation** — the current 12MF ~22x is somewhat higher than peers (14–18x), but a −27% correction from the June high has brought it below the Base target price (+39.1%). If new revenue falls short of expectations, re-correction risk remains.
- **Net-profit volatility** — large FX/financial-income swings cause net profit to fluctuate sharply versus operating profit (even at 14% OPM in 2025, net profit fell YoY).
- **Customer concentration** — dependence on a few large customers such as Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix. Revenue is sensitive to customer investment cycles and inventory adjustments.
- **Restructuring in progress** — the Chinese-subsidiary sale and blowing-agent separation are underway; P&L and cash flow may vary with the terms and timing of the sale.

## 7. Conclusion

_Proven core business + unproven EUV/HBM options_

Dongjin Semichem is Korea's only volume photoresist maker, a rare stock being reshaped — through the blowing-agent separation and China sale — into a pure-play semiconductor/display materials company. With the semiconductor cycle recovery, the existing core business is already in an improving phase.

That said, the current price of 43,050 KRW (12MF ~22x, somewhat higher than peers 14–18x) has, on a correction from the June high, begun to sit below the value of the existing core business. The bottom-up Base target price exceeds the current price by +39.1%, and further upside (Bull +60.3% · Best +92.1%) opens only when new EUV photoresist / HBM CMP revenue actually prints in earnings. That is, it is a structure of buying a multiple that layers "unproven EUV/HBM options" on a "proven core business," and a phased approach while confirming option realization is reasonable.

1회사 개요

설립·분할 상장 · 실적 개요 · 사업 구성

솔브레인은 반도체·디스플레이·2차전지 공정에 들어가는 화학 소재를 만드는 회사임. 2020년 7월 솔브레인홀딩스에서 소재 제조부문이 인적분할돼 신설됐고, 같은 해 8월 코스닥에 상장함.

2025년 연결 매출은 9,234억원으로, 반도체 부문이 7,670억원(83%)을 차지하는 사실상 반도체 소재 기업임. 디스플레이 647억원(7%), 2차전지 605억원(7%), 기타 313억원(3%)이 뒤를 잇는 구조임.

주력은 고순도 불산(HF) 계열 식각액과 BOE, CMP 슬러리, ALD/CVD용 전구체(Precursor), 고순도 인산임. 삼성전자·SK하이닉스 메모리 라인에 안정적으로 공급하며 국산화를 주도해 온 소재 국산화의 대표 기업임.

2025년은 매출이 늘었으나 순이익이 791억원으로 전년 1,184억원 대비 −33% 급감함. 다만 2026년 1분기 매출이 +25.9% YoY, 영업이익률 16.9%로 반등하며 실적 회복 국면에 진입함.

3.1.반도체 공정소재의 작동 원리

반도체는 웨이퍼 위에 회로를 수백 번 그렸다 깎아내며 만들어짐. 이때 필요 없는 막을 화학약품으로 녹여 없애는 것이 식각(Etching), 깎인 면을 거울처럼 평탄하게 가는 것이 연마(CMP), 원하는 막을 한 층씩 입히는 것이 증착(Deposition)임.

솔브레인의 식각액(HF·인산 계열)은 식각 공정에서 특정 막만 골라 녹이는 약품임. 선폭이 미세해질수록 원하는 막만 정확히 녹이는 선택비가 중요해져, 소재 순도와 배합 기술이 경쟁력이 됨.

CMP 슬러리는 연마 공정에서 쓰는 미세 입자 연마액으로, 표면을 원자 단위로 평탄화함. 전구체는 증착 공정에서 막의 원료가 되는 화학물질로, 고유전율(High-k) 막이나 3D 구조 형성에 필수임.

이 소재들은 한 번 검증되면 공정에 깊이 맞물려 쉽게 바뀌지 않는 lock-in 성격을 가짐. 동시에 불량이 곧 수율 손실로 직결돼, 고객은 검증된 소수 공급사에 의존함. 이것이 솔브레인 같은 국산화 선도 업체의 해자임.

3.2.시장 구조와 솔브레인의 위치

솔브레인의 핵심 무기는 고순도 불산 식각액과 고순도 인산에서의 국내 1위 지위임. 과거 일본 스텔라케미파 등이 장악하던 영역을 국산화로 대체하며 삼성전자·SK하이닉스의 주력 공급사로 자리잡음.

2019년 일본의 불화수소 수출 규제는 역설적으로 솔브레인에 기회가 됐음. 12나인급 액체 불화수소 국산화에 성공하며 국내 액체 HF 물량의 70~80%를 담당하는 공급사로 입지를 굳힘.

식각액 글로벌 경쟁사는 일본 스텔라케미파·모리타화학·다이킨, 국내 이엔에프테크놀로지·후성임. 단 솔브레인홀딩스는 2024년 스텔라케미파와 자본·업무 제휴를 맺어 원조 강자와 경쟁이자 협력 관계로 전환함(고순도 불산 합작사 훽트 지분 솔브레인 49%·스텔라 39% 공동 보유).

CMP 슬러리·전구체는 Entegris·Merck 등 글로벌 강자가 우위이나, 솔브레인이 국산화로 점유를 넓혀가는 영역임. 단일 소재가 아니라 식각·연마·증착을 아우르는 종합 소재사라는 점이 차별점임.

3.3.미세화·단수 증가가 만드는 수요

반도체 소재의 성장은 가격(P)이 아니라 물량(Q)에서 나옴. 소재 판가는 연 +6% 수준으로 인상폭이 제한적이나, 고객이 소비하는 소재의 절대량이 공정 복잡도에 따라 늘어남.

NAND는 층을 위로 쌓는 3D 구조로, 단수가 200단·300단·400단으로 높아질수록 깊은 구멍을 뚫는 식각 공정 횟수와 식각액 사용량이 비례해 늘어남. 단수 증가가 곧 식각액 수요 증가임.

DRAM은 미세화와 HBM(고대역폭메모리) 적층으로 식각·세정·연마 단계가 늘고, HBM의 TSV(실리콘 관통 전극) 공정은 추가 식각·CMP 수요를 만듦. 메모리가 미세화·고적층으로 갈수록 웨이퍼 1장당 소재 소비량이 증가하는 구조임.

핵심 구조: 솔브레인은 메모리 capex 사이클에 연동되는 'Q 레버리지' 종목임. 삼성·SK의 wafer 투입량과 공정 step 수가 늘면 소재 물량이 비례해 증가함. 단가 인상보다 NAND 단수·DRAM 미세화·HBM 적층에 따른 물량 증가가 실적의 본질적 동인임.

4.1.매출 구조 — 부문별

매출의 83%가 반도체 소재에서 나오는 사실상 단일 부문 구조임. 반도체 매출은 2023년 6,070억원에서 2025년 7,670억원으로 2년간 +26% 성장하며 전사 실적을 견인함.

디스플레이·2차전지는 각각 7%로 비중이 작고 성장도 둔화돼 있음. 디스플레이는 OLED 식각·박형 글라스 가공, 2차전지는 전해액이 주력이나 전기차 수요 둔화로 부진함.

2026년 1분기에는 반도체 비중이 84%로 더 높아짐(2,219억원). 반도체 의존이 심화되는 동시에, 반도체 업황 회복이 곧 전사 실적 회복으로 직결되는 구조임.

4.2.고객집중도

고객이 삼성전자·SK하이닉스 두 메모리 대기업에 집중된 구조임. 이는 안정적 거래 기반이자 동시에 두 고객의 capex 사이클에 실적이 묶이는 집중도 리스크임.

소재는 판가 협상력이 제한적이나, 검증·lock-in으로 거래가 장기 지속됨. 삼성전자가 지분 5.62%를 보유한 점은 단순 거래를 넘어선 전략적 결속을 보여줌.

TSMC·인텔·마이크론 같은 해외 IDM·파운드리는 직접 고객이 아님. 솔브레인의 해외 진출도 삼성전자 미국 테일러 파운드리를 따라간 것으로, 고객을 좇는 구조이지 외국 고객을 독자 확보한 것은 아님. 다만 텍사스 인산 공장은 비용 급등·물량 불확실로 착공이 2028년으로 보류됨.

6밸류에이션

6.1 적용 PER · 6.2 시나리오 분석 · 6.3 시나리오 서사

매출 추정 방식은 탑다운(시장규모 × 점유율)으로 통일함. 제품별 물량·단가가 미공시라 P×Q 직접 분해가 불가하므로, 반도체 부문은 솔브레인 가용시장(SAM) × 점유율로 산출하고 디스플레이·2차전지·기타는 직접 추정함.

1Company Overview

Founding & spin-off listing · earnings snapshot · business mix

Soulbrain makes the chemical materials used in semiconductor, display, and secondary-battery processes. In July 2020 its materials-manufacturing division was spun off from Soulbrain Holdings to establish the new company, which listed on KOSDAQ in August of the same year.

2025 consolidated revenue was 923.4bn KRW, with the semiconductor segment accounting for 767.0bn (83%) — effectively a semiconductor-materials company. Display 64.7bn (7%), secondary batteries 60.5bn (7%), and other 31.3bn (3%) follow.

Its mainstays are high-purity hydrofluoric-acid (HF)-based etchants and BOE, CMP slurry, ALD/CVD precursors, and high-purity phosphoric acid. It supplies Samsung Electronics and SK hynix memory lines stably and is the representative materials-localization company that has led localization.

In 2025 revenue rose but net profit plunged −33% to 79.1bn KRW from 118.4bn a year earlier. That said, 1Q26 revenue rebounded +25.9% YoY with a 16.9% operating margin, entering an earnings-recovery phase.

3Industry Analysis

3.1 How it works · 3.2 Market structure & position · 3.3 Scaling & stack-count demand

We examine what semiconductor process materials are and why they are needed (principles) → Soulbrain's market position and share (structure) → the demand drivers created by memory scaling and rising stack counts (external variables), in that order.

3.1.How Semiconductor Process Materials Work

Semiconductors are made by drawing and etching away circuits on a wafer hundreds of times. Dissolving away unwanted films with chemicals is etching, grinding the cut surface mirror-flat is CMP (polishing), and depositing the desired films one layer at a time is deposition.

Soulbrain's etchants (HF/phosphoric-acid based) are chemicals that selectively dissolve only specific films in the etching process. As line widths shrink, the selectivity to dissolve only the intended film becomes more important, so material purity and formulation technology become the competitive edge.

CMP slurry is a fine-particle polishing liquid used in the polishing process, planarizing the surface at the atomic scale. Precursors are the chemicals that serve as the raw material of films in the deposition process, essential for forming high-k films and 3D structures.

Once validated, these materials have a lock-in character — deeply meshed with the process and not easily changed. At the same time, a defect translates directly into yield loss, so customers rely on a validated few suppliers. This is the moat of a localization leader like Soulbrain.

3.2.Market Structure & Soulbrain's Position

Soulbrain's core weapon is its domestic No. 1 position in high-purity HF etchants and high-purity phosphoric acid. Replacing via localization an area once dominated by Japan's Stella Chemifa and others, it established itself as a main supplier to Samsung Electronics and SK hynix.

Japan's 2019 hydrogen-fluoride export restrictions paradoxically became an opportunity for Soulbrain. Succeeding in localizing 12-nine-grade liquid hydrogen fluoride, it cemented its position as the supplier handling 70–80% of domestic liquid-HF volume.

Global etchant rivals are Japan's Stella Chemifa, Morita Chemical, and Daikin, and domestically ENF Technology and Foosung. However, Soulbrain Holdings entered a capital/business alliance with Stella Chemifa in 2024, turning the relationship with the original heavyweight into one of both competition and cooperation (in the high-purity HF joint venture Foecht, Soulbrain holds 49% and Stella 39% jointly).

In CMP slurry and precursors, global heavyweights like Entegris and Merck hold the advantage, but this is an area where Soulbrain is expanding share via localization. Its differentiation is being a comprehensive materials company spanning etching, polishing, and deposition rather than a single material.

3.3.Demand Created by Scaling & Rising Stack Counts

Growth in semiconductor materials comes not from price (P) but from volume (Q). Material selling prices rise only modestly, around +6% a year, but the absolute quantity of materials customers consume grows with process complexity.

NAND is a 3D structure stacked upward, and as stack counts rise to 200, 300, and 400 layers, the number of etching steps to drill deep holes and the amount of etchant used increase proportionally. Rising stack counts mean rising etchant demand.

DRAM adds etching, cleaning, and polishing steps through scaling and HBM (high-bandwidth memory) stacking, and HBM's TSV (through-silicon-via) process creates additional etching/CMP demand. The more memory moves toward scaling and high-stacking, the more materials are consumed per wafer.

Core structure: Soulbrain is a "Q-leverage" name tied to the memory capex cycle. As Samsung's and SK's wafer inputs and process step counts increase, material volume grows proportionally. More than price hikes, volume growth from NAND stack counts, DRAM scaling, and HBM stacking is the essential earnings driver.

4Business Model

4.1 Revenue mix · 4.2 Customer concentration

4.1.Revenue Mix — By Segment

With 83% of revenue coming from semiconductor materials, it is effectively a single-segment structure. Semiconductor revenue grew +26% over two years from 607.0bn KRW in 2023 to 767.0bn in 2025, driving company-wide results.

Display and secondary batteries are small at 7% each and their growth has slowed. Display centers on OLED etching and thin-glass processing, and secondary batteries on electrolytes, but the latter is sluggish amid slowing EV demand.

In 1Q26 the semiconductor share rose further to 84% (221.9bn KRW). Semiconductor dependence deepens while, at the same time, a semiconductor-industry recovery translates directly into a company-wide earnings recovery.

4.2.Customer Concentration

Customers are concentrated in the two memory giants, Samsung Electronics and SK hynix. This is both a stable transaction base and, at the same time, a concentration risk with earnings tied to the two customers' capex cycles.

Materials have limited pricing power, but transactions persist long-term through validation and lock-in. Samsung Electronics' 5.62% stake shows a strategic bond beyond simple transactions.

Overseas IDMs and foundries like TSMC, Intel, and Micron are not direct customers. Soulbrain's overseas expansion also followed Samsung Electronics' Taylor, U.S. foundry — a structure of chasing the customer rather than independently securing foreign customers. That said, the Texas phosphoric-acid plant's groundbreaking has been deferred to 2028 amid surging costs and volume uncertainty.

6.3.Scenario Narratives

Base — Materials-market CAGR 7.4% + share held

Semiconductor SAM grows 7.4% a year from $9.7bn in 2025 to $12bn in FY28, with Soulbrain's share held at 5.9%. Semiconductor revenue 955.8bn · total revenue 1,123.8bn · 16% OPM gives EPS 18,030 KRW, and at a 20x P/E a target price of 360,600 KRW (+28.1%).

A conservative baseline in which memory capex grows on trend and Soulbrain holds its current share. The current price sits 28% below this — a zone that has retraced the June spike and dropped below Base.

Bull — AI capex acceleration + share expansion

With AI-memory capex acceleration, materials SAM grows 11% a year (to $13.3bn), and with higher NAND stack counts and new-material penetration, share rises to 6.6%. Semiconductor revenue 11,850 → 1,185.0bn · 18% OPM gives EPS 24,960 KRW · target price 499,200 KRW (+77.4%).

A scenario in which market growth and share expansion proceed together — the Bull target price is +77% above the current price. The 52-week high (530,000 KRW) corresponds to around this level.

Best — Memory super-cycle + localization/foundry share

With a memory super-cycle, materials SAM surges 15% a year (to $14.8bn), and with accelerating localization and Samsung foundry materials share, share rises to 7.3% — the upside case. Semiconductor revenue 1,458.5bn · 20% OPM gives EPS 33,930 KRW · target price 678,500 KRW (+141.0%).

A structural-inflection scenario in which market expansion and share gains occur simultaneously and company-wide revenue reaches 1.7tn KRW. If memory capex falls below consensus or share stalls, it could revert to the low end of Base.

7Conclusion

Q-leverage materials stock · near-term valuation burden

Soulbrain is a "Q-leverage" materials name whose material volume (Q) grows with memory scaling, rising NAND stack counts, and HBM stacking, holding the moat of domestic No. 1 in high-purity etchants. Earnings are tied to the memory capex cycle rather than to price (P).

Customers are concentrated in Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, and its overseas expansion also chases the customer, so it has no record of independently securing foreign IDMs like TSMC or Intel. The Texas phosphoric-acid plant is also deferred to 2028.

At 281,500 KRW, the current price has retraced its June spike and sits 28% below the Base target price (~361,000 KRW). The Bull (+77%) and Best (+141%) upside is premised on confirmation of a memory super-cycle and share expansion.

The fundamentals are solid, but a near-term valuation burden coexists. Watching for the persistence of the semiconductor segment's quarterly growth (1Q26 +26%) and whether display/secondary batteries recover, phased accumulation on an approach to the Base low end (~361,000 KRW) is reasonable.
